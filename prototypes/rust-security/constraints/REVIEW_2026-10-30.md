>=1.0.0-alpha.1 as patched. GitHub's machine range still has introduced0,
fixed1.0.0-alpha.1, while its informational last-known-affected range is<=0.10.3.

confirms that its Vec<T> decoder rejects size_of::<T>()==0 before parsing length,
replacing the older unsafe synthesized-vector path. The bounded published-backport
probe confirms rejection for three small lengths and ordinary non-ZST decoding.
**The older vulnerable0.10.3 implementation is inspected, never executed.**

This supports a primary-data mismatch; it is not a local authorization to dismiss
the finding. No severity/range filter, scanner exception, advisory replacement or
reachability exemption is added. Trivy remains red pending authoritative data
reconciliation or a fully qualified dependency change. No upstream message or
issue has been sent. A financial major upgrade is not justified solely by the
scanner's conflicting fixed-version field.
